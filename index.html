About

I study how infectious disease surveillance and inference can be improved when infection processes are only partially observed. My work focuses on wildlife and multi-host systems, using spatiotemporal epidemiology, Bayesian methods, and mechanistic models to understand transmission processes and support better surveillance decisions.

Research interests

  • Surveillance design and inference under imperfect observation
  • Spatiotemporal epidemiology of wildlife and multi-host infectious diseases
  • Mechanistic modelling of infectious disease transmission

Current focus: infectious disease surveillance under imperfect observation and wildlife–multi-host disease systems under environmental change.
