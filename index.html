About

I study infectious disease transmission, spillover, and surveillance across human, livestock, and wildlife systems. My work uses statistical, spatial, and mechanistic models to understand disease dynamics and improve surveillance under imperfect observation.

Research interests

  • Wildlife and multi-host infectious disease epidemiology
  • Surveillance design and evaluation under imperfect observation
  • Spatial and spatiotemporal epidemiology
  • Bayesian and mechanistic modelling
  • Zoonotic and vector-borne diseases
